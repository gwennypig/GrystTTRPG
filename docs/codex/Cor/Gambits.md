# Gambits

Gambits are actions with pre-authored outcomes. Each has four possible results: Triumphus (critical success), Successus (success), Clades (failure), and Calamitas (critical failure).

---

## Supporting Fire

> Choose a SIMPLEX TELUM gambit. You gain X+1 stacks of [[SUPPORTING FIRE]] where 0<X<=5.
Spend 2X MTM. After every time a party member chooses a gambit, you may spend one stack of [[SUPPORTING FIRE]] to ADDON your chosen gambit with a 0.5 MAG modifier. This instance of the gambit creates an {{dict:OCCĀSIŌTVRBIDA}} upon SuTr.

**Add-On Cost:** —

**Tags:** [CARDINALIS]/Pivotal, [ABSOLUTUM]/Absolute, [TELUM]/Missile

### Outcomes

**Triumphus (Critical Success):** Critical success!

**Successus (Success):** You succeed.

**Clades (Failure):** You fail.

**Calamitas (Critical Failure):** Critical failure!
