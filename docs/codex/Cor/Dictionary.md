# Dictionary

Common terms and concepts used throughout the rules. These are referenced inline in rules text.

---

## <span style="color:#ff3333">ACCRVE</span> / Accrue

During the [<span style="color:#ff3333;font-weight:600">PHASEACCRETIALIS</span>](./Dictionary.md#phaseaccretialis-accrual-phase), [<span style="color:#e2725b;font-weight:600">PERSONA</span>](./Dictionary.md#persona-character)e with agency simultaneously gain [<span style="color:#ff3333;font-weight:600">MOMENTVM</span>](./Dictionary.md#momentvm-mtm).

## <span style="color:#43b3ae">ADDENDVM</span> / Addon

An [<span style="color:#43b3ae;font-weight:600">ADDENDVM</span>](./Dictionary.md#addendvm-addon) is a gambit added during the [<span style="color:#43b3ae;font-weight:600">ADDENDVM</span>](./Dictionary.md#addendvm-addon) window. By paying the [<span style="color:#43b3ae;font-weight:600">ADDENDVM</span>](./Dictionary.md#addendvm-addon) cost of a [<span style="color:#70e697;font-weight:600">GAMBIT</span>](./Dictionary.md#gambit), a [<span style="color:#e2725b;font-weight:600">PERSONA</span>](./Dictionary.md#persona-character) may jump into the fray in support of their [<span style="color:#66023c;font-weight:600">COHORS</span>](./Dictionary.md#cohors-cohort).

## <span style="color:#ff0000">CALAMITAS</span> / Calamity

Your [<span style="color:#70e697;font-weight:600">GAMBIT</span>](./Dictionary.md#gambit) hasn't just [<span style="color:#8c0303;font-weight:600">CLADES</span>](./Dictionary.md#clades-failure)'d. It has [<span style="color:#ff0000;font-weight:600">CALAMITAS</span>](./Dictionary.md#calamitas-calamity)'d. You or your [<span style="color:#66023c;font-weight:600">COHORS</span>](./Dictionary.md#cohors-cohort) are worse than when you started.

## <span style="color:#ffd500">CARDINALIS</span> / Pivotal

The [<span style="color:#ffd500;font-weight:600">CARDINALIS</span>](./Dictionary.md#cardinalis-pivotal) character sets the scene. They have access to [<span style="color:#0004ff;font-weight:600">[CARDINALIS]</span>](./Tags.md#cardinalis-pivotal) [<span style="color:#70e697;font-weight:600">GAMBIT</span>](./Dictionary.md#gambit)s and initialize the [<span style="color:#708090;font-weight:600">TABVLA</span>](./Dictionary.md#tabvla-queue) upon selecting a [<span style="color:#70e697;font-weight:600">GAMBIT</span>](./Dictionary.md#gambit).

## <span style="color:#9d8015">CHORAGVS</span> / DM

The Dungeon Master. During Roman times, the [<span style="color:#9d8015;font-weight:600">CHORAGVS</span>](./Dictionary.md#choragvs-dm) was the patron of the Roman art of theatre. This person, known by a Greek-derived title, oversaw the entire logistics and production of the [<span style="color:#dc143c;font-weight:600">FABVLA</span>](./Dictionary.md#fabvla-campaign).

## <span style="color:#000000">CHORAGVS PERSONAEQVE GRYSTI</span> / CPQG

The [<span style="color:#9d8015;font-weight:600">CHORAGVS</span>](./Dictionary.md#choragvs-dm) and [<span style="color:#e2725b;font-weight:600">PERSONA</span>](./Dictionary.md#persona-character)e of Gryst.
Search for "SPQR" if you are not familiar with the term. This is the table and all concerned parties.

## <span style="color:#8c0303">CLADES</span> / Failure

You have failed in your [<span style="color:#70e697;font-weight:600">GAMBIT</span>](./Dictionary.md#gambit).

## <span style="color:#ff0000">ClCa</span>

If something is conditional on [<span style="color:#ff0000;font-weight:600">ClCa</span>](./Dictionary.md#clca), it is conditional on either [<span style="color:#8c0303;font-weight:600">CLADES</span>](./Dictionary.md#clades-failure) or [<span style="color:#ff0000;font-weight:600">CALAMITAS</span>](./Dictionary.md#calamitas-calamity).

## <span style="color:#66023c">COHORS</span> / Cohort

Any group of [<span style="color:#e2725b;font-weight:600">PERSONA</span>](./Dictionary.md#persona-character)e with [<span style="color:#66023c;font-weight:600">CONCORDIA</span>](./Dictionary.md#concordia-cooperation) amongst themselves become a [<span style="color:#66023c;font-weight:600">COHORS</span>](./Dictionary.md#cohors-cohort).
This is the "party" in Gryst. [<span style="color:#e2725b;font-weight:600">PERSONA</span>](./Dictionary.md#persona-character)e within a [<span style="color:#66023c;font-weight:600">COHORS</span>](./Dictionary.md#cohors-cohort) may freely contribute during the [<span style="color:#43b3ae;font-weight:600">ADDENDVM</span>](./Dictionary.md#addendvm-addon) window without needing [<span style="color:#9d64f2;font-weight:600">[INTERVENTVS]</span>](./Tags.md#interventvs-intervention) or an [<span style="color:#9d64f2;font-weight:600">OCCĀSIŌTVRBIDA</span>](./Dictionary.md#occ-si-tvrbida-chaotic-moment).

## <span style="color:#66023c">CONCORDIA</span> / Cooperation

[<span style="color:#e2725b;font-weight:600">PERSONA</span>](./Dictionary.md#persona-character)e with [<span style="color:#66023c;font-weight:600">CONCORDIA</span>](./Dictionary.md#concordia-cooperation) may freely contribute during the [<span style="color:#43b3ae;font-weight:600">ADDENDVM</span>](./Dictionary.md#addendvm-addon) window without needing [<span style="color:#9d64f2;font-weight:600">[INTERVENTVS]</span>](./Tags.md#interventvs-intervention) or an [<span style="color:#9d64f2;font-weight:600">OCCĀSIŌTVRBIDA</span>](./Dictionary.md#occ-si-tvrbida-chaotic-moment).

## <span style="color:#e770ff">DESIGNA</span> / Designate(d)

[<span style="color:#e770ff;font-weight:600">DESIGNA</span>](./Dictionary.md#designa-designate-d)te a [<span style="color:#e2725b;font-weight:600">PERSONA</span>](./Dictionary.md#persona-character), [<span style="color:#64e1f2;font-weight:600">MODULUS</span>](./Dictionary.md#modulus-module), LOCULUS, et cetera. When a player is told to [<span style="color:#e770ff;font-weight:600">DESIGNA</span>](./Dictionary.md#designa-designate-d)te, they are making a choice, such as what a [<span style="color:#70e697;font-weight:600">GAMBIT</span>](./Dictionary.md#gambit), is "targeting".

## <span style="color:#dc143c">FABVLA</span> / Campaign

The story that the [<span style="color:#000000;font-weight:600">CHORAGVS PERSONAEQVE GRYSTI</span>](./Dictionary.md#choragvs-personaeqve-grysti-cpqg) is trying to tell. It is the campaign that we have embarked on. The story waiting to unfold.

## <span style="color:#70e697">GAMBIT</span>

A [<span style="color:#70e697;font-weight:600">GAMBIT</span>](./Dictionary.md#gambit) is the atomic unit of resolution. Any action that requires mechanical resolution is a [<span style="color:#70e697;font-weight:600">GAMBIT</span>](./Dictionary.md#gambit). Most [<span style="color:#70e697;font-weight:600">GAMBIT</span>](./Dictionary.md#gambit)s can have 1 of 4 outcomes; [<span style="color:#00ff7b;font-weight:600">SVCCVSSVS</span>](./Dictionary.md#svccvssvs-success) (success), [<span style="color:#8c0303;font-weight:600">CLADES</span>](./Dictionary.md#clades-failure) (failure), [<span style="color:#ffea00;font-weight:600">TRIVMPHVS</span>](./Dictionary.md#trivmphvs-triumph) (critical success), or [<span style="color:#ff0000;font-weight:600">CALAMITAS</span>](./Dictionary.md#calamitas-calamity) (critical failure).

## <span style="color:#ff3333">MOMENTVM</span> / MTM

[<span style="color:#ff3333;font-weight:600">MOMENTVM</span>](./Dictionary.md#momentvm-mtm) is a resource that [<span style="color:#ff3333;font-weight:600">ACCRVE</span>](./Dictionary.md#accrve-accrue)s during the [<span style="color:#ff3333;font-weight:600">PHASEACCRETIALIS</span>](./Dictionary.md#phaseaccretialis-accrual-phase). Upon reaching 20 MTM, that [<span style="color:#e2725b;font-weight:600">PERSONA</span>](./Dictionary.md#persona-character) becomes the [<span style="color:#e2725b;font-weight:600">PERSONA</span>](./Dictionary.md#persona-character) [<span style="color:#ffd500;font-weight:600">CARDINALIS</span>](./Dictionary.md#cardinalis-pivotal) and their [<span style="color:#66023c;font-weight:600">COHORS</span>](./Dictionary.md#cohors-cohort) becomes the [<span style="color:#66023c;font-weight:600">COHORS</span>](./Dictionary.md#cohors-cohort) [<span style="color:#ffd500;font-weight:600">CARDINALIS</span>](./Dictionary.md#cardinalis-pivotal).
It is also spent during the [<span style="color:#43b3ae;font-weight:600">ADDENDVM</span>](./Dictionary.md#addendvm-addon) window to make [<span style="color:#43b3ae;font-weight:600">ADDENDVM</span>](./Dictionary.md#addendvm-addon)s to the [<span style="color:#708090;font-weight:600">TABVLA</span>](./Dictionary.md#tabvla-queue).It is simultaneously the "turn order" (sic) and Action Economy of Gryst.

## <span style="color:#9d64f2">OCCĀSIŌTVRBIDA</span> / Chaotic Moment

During an [<span style="color:#9d64f2;font-weight:600">OCCĀSIŌTVRBIDA</span>](./Dictionary.md#occ-si-tvrbida-chaotic-moment) (chaotic moment), the monopoly on action of the [<span style="color:#66023c;font-weight:600">COHORS</span>](./Dictionary.md#cohors-cohort) [<span style="color:#ffd500;font-weight:600">CARDINALIS</span>](./Dictionary.md#cardinalis-pivotal) has been broken. The chaos has allowed outside [<span style="color:#66023c;font-weight:600">COHORS</span>](./Dictionary.md#cohors-cohort) to jump in on the action!

## <span style="color:#e2725b">PERSONA</span> / Character

[<span style="color:#e2725b;font-weight:600">PERSONA</span>](./Dictionary.md#persona-character)e are the characters within our [<span style="color:#dc143c;font-weight:600">FABVLA</span>](./Dictionary.md#fabvla-campaign). They can be controlled by players or the [<span style="color:#9d8015;font-weight:600">CHORAGVS</span>](./Dictionary.md#choragvs-dm). They can act alone or in a [<span style="color:#66023c;font-weight:600">COHORS</span>](./Dictionary.md#cohors-cohort) with [<span style="color:#66023c;font-weight:600">CONCORDIA</span>](./Dictionary.md#concordia-cooperation).

## <span style="color:#ff3333">PHASEACCRETIALIS</span> / Accrual Phase

All characters [<span style="color:#ff3333;font-weight:600">ACCRVE</span>](./Dictionary.md#accrve-accrue) [<span style="color:#ff3333;font-weight:600">MOMENTVM</span>](./Dictionary.md#momentvm-mtm) during this phase. Upon reaching 20 [<span style="color:#ff3333;font-weight:600">MOMENTVM</span>](./Dictionary.md#momentvm-mtm) and winning any required tiebreakers, that [<span style="color:#e2725b;font-weight:600">PERSONA</span>](./Dictionary.md#persona-character) becomes the [<span style="color:#e2725b;font-weight:600">PERSONA</span>](./Dictionary.md#persona-character) [<span style="color:#ffd500;font-weight:600">CARDINALIS</span>](./Dictionary.md#cardinalis-pivotal), setting the scene for the current immediate moment in this [<span style="color:#68001a;font-weight:600">SCAENA</span>](./Dictionary.md#scaena-scene), initializing the [<span style="color:#708090;font-weight:600">TABVLA</span>](./Dictionary.md#tabvla-queue).
