# Dictionary

Common terms and concepts used throughout the rules. These are referenced inline in rules text.

## <span style="color:#ff3333">ACCRVE</span> / Accrue

During the [<span style="color:#ff3333;font-weight:600">PHASEACCRETIALIS</span>](/codex/Cor/Dictionary?id=phaseaccretialis-accrual-phase), [<span style="color:#e2725b;font-weight:600">PERSONA</span>](/codex/Cor/Dictionary?id=persona-character)e with agency simultaneously gain [<span style="color:#ff3333;font-weight:600">MOMENTVM</span>](/codex/Cor/Dictionary?id=momentvm-mtm).

## <span style="color:#43b3ae">ADDENDVM</span> / Addon

An [<span style="color:#43b3ae;font-weight:600">ADDENDVM</span>](/codex/Cor/Dictionary?id=addendvm-addon) is a gambit added during the [<span style="color:#43b3ae;font-weight:600">ADDENDVM</span>](/codex/Cor/Dictionary?id=addendvm-addon) window. By paying the [<span style="color:#43b3ae;font-weight:600">ADDENDVM</span>](/codex/Cor/Dictionary?id=addendvm-addon) cost of a [<span style="color:#70e697;font-weight:600">GAMBIT</span>](/codex/Cor/Dictionary?id=gambit), a [<span style="color:#e2725b;font-weight:600">PERSONA</span>](/codex/Cor/Dictionary?id=persona-character) may jump into the fray in support of their [<span style="color:#66023c;font-weight:600">COHORS</span>](/codex/Cor/Dictionary?id=cohors-cohort).

## <span style="color:#ff0000">CALAMITAS</span> / Calamity

Your [<span style="color:#70e697;font-weight:600">GAMBIT</span>](/codex/Cor/Dictionary?id=gambit) hasn't just [<span style="color:#8c0303;font-weight:600">CLADES</span>](/codex/Cor/Dictionary?id=clades-failure)'d. It has [<span style="color:#ff0000;font-weight:600">CALAMITAS</span>](/codex/Cor/Dictionary?id=calamitas-calamity)'d. You or your [<span style="color:#66023c;font-weight:600">COHORS</span>](/codex/Cor/Dictionary?id=cohors-cohort) are worse than when you started.

## <span style="color:#ffd500">CARDINALIS</span> / Pivotal

The [<span style="color:#ffd500;font-weight:600">CARDINALIS</span>](/codex/Cor/Dictionary?id=cardinalis-pivotal) character sets the scene. They have access to [<span style="color:#0004ff;font-weight:600">[CARDINALIS]</span>](/codex/Cor/Tags?id=cardinalis-pivotal) [<span style="color:#70e697;font-weight:600">GAMBIT</span>](/codex/Cor/Dictionary?id=gambit)s and initialize the [<span style="color:#708090;font-weight:600">TABVLA</span>](/codex/Cor/Dictionary?id=tabvla-queue) upon selecting a [<span style="color:#70e697;font-weight:600">GAMBIT</span>](/codex/Cor/Dictionary?id=gambit).

## <span style="color:#aeff00">CaTr</span> / Cater

If something is conditional on [<span style="color:#aeff00;font-weight:600">CaTr</span>](/codex/Cor/Dictionary?id=catr-cater), it is conditional on either [<span style="color:#ff0000;font-weight:600">CALAMITAS</span>](/codex/Cor/Dictionary?id=calamitas-calamity) or [<span style="color:#ffea00;font-weight:600">TRIVMPHVS</span>](/codex/Cor/Dictionary?id=trivmphvs-triumph)
This represents the extraordinary range.

## <span style="color:#9d8015">CHORAGVS</span> / DM

The Dungeon Master. During Roman times, the [<span style="color:#9d8015;font-weight:600">CHORAGVS</span>](/codex/Cor/Dictionary?id=choragvs-dm) was the patron of the Roman art of theatre. This person, known by a Greek-derived title, oversaw the entire logistics and production of the [<span style="color:#dc143c;font-weight:600">FABVLA</span>](/codex/Cor/Dictionary?id=fabvla-campaign).

## <span style="color:#000000">CHORAGVS PERSONAEQVE GRYSTI</span> / CPQG

The [<span style="color:#9d8015;font-weight:600">CHORAGVS</span>](/codex/Cor/Dictionary?id=choragvs-dm) and [<span style="color:#e2725b;font-weight:600">PERSONA</span>](/codex/Cor/Dictionary?id=persona-character)e of Gryst.
Search for "SPQR" if you are not familiar with the term. This is the table and all concerned parties.

## <span style="color:#ff0000">ClCa</span> / Culca

## <span style="color:#7b7b7b">ClSv</span> / Culsu

## <span style="color:#66023c">COHORS</span> / Cohort

Any group of [<span style="color:#e2725b;font-weight:600">PERSONA</span>](/codex/Cor/Dictionary?id=persona-character)e with [<span style="color:#66023c;font-weight:600">CONCORDIA</span>](/codex/Cor/Dictionary?id=concordia-cooperation) amongst themselves become a [<span style="color:#66023c;font-weight:600">COHORS</span>](/codex/Cor/Dictionary?id=cohors-cohort).
This is the "party" in Gryst. [<span style="color:#e2725b;font-weight:600">PERSONA</span>](/codex/Cor/Dictionary?id=persona-character)e within a [<span style="color:#66023c;font-weight:600">COHORS</span>](/codex/Cor/Dictionary?id=cohors-cohort) may freely contribute during the [<span style="color:#43b3ae;font-weight:600">ADDENDVM</span>](/codex/Cor/Dictionary?id=addendvm-addon) window without needing [<span style="color:#9d64f2;font-weight:600">[INTERVENTVS]</span>](/codex/Cor/Tags?id=interventvs-intervention) or an [<span style="color:#9d64f2;font-weight:600">OCCĀSIŌTVRBIDA</span>](/codex/Cor/Dictionary?id=occĀsiŌtvrbida-chaotic-moment).

## <span style="color:#66023c">CONCORDIA</span> / Cooperation

[<span style="color:#e2725b;font-weight:600">PERSONA</span>](/codex/Cor/Dictionary?id=persona-character)e with [<span style="color:#66023c;font-weight:600">CONCORDIA</span>](/codex/Cor/Dictionary?id=concordia-cooperation) may freely contribute during the [<span style="color:#43b3ae;font-weight:600">ADDENDVM</span>](/codex/Cor/Dictionary?id=addendvm-addon) window without needing [<span style="color:#9d64f2;font-weight:600">[INTERVENTVS]</span>](/codex/Cor/Tags?id=interventvs-intervention) or an [<span style="color:#9d64f2;font-weight:600">OCCĀSIŌTVRBIDA</span>](/codex/Cor/Dictionary?id=occĀsiŌtvrbida-chaotic-moment).

## <span style="color:#dc143c">FABVLA</span> / Campaign

The story that the [<span style="color:#000000;font-weight:600">CHORAGVS PERSONAEQVE GRYSTI</span>](/codex/Cor/Dictionary?id=choragvs-personaeqve-grysti-cpqg) is trying to tell. It is the campaign that we have embarked on. The story waiting to unfold.

## <span style="color:#70e697">GAMBIT</span>

A [<span style="color:#70e697;font-weight:600">GAMBIT</span>](/codex/Cor/Dictionary?id=gambit) is the atomic unit of resolution. Any action that requires mechanical resolution is a [<span style="color:#70e697;font-weight:600">GAMBIT</span>](/codex/Cor/Dictionary?id=gambit). Most [<span style="color:#70e697;font-weight:600">GAMBIT</span>](/codex/Cor/Dictionary?id=gambit)s can have 1 of 4 outcomes; [<span style="color:#00ff7b;font-weight:600">SVCCVSSVS</span>](/codex/Cor/Dictionary?id=svccvssvs-success) (success), [<span style="color:#8c0303;font-weight:600">CLADES</span>](/codex/Cor/Dictionary?id=clades-failure) (failure), [<span style="color:#ffea00;font-weight:600">TRIVMPHVS</span>](/codex/Cor/Dictionary?id=trivmphvs-triumph) (critical success), or [<span style="color:#ff0000;font-weight:600">CALAMITAS</span>](/codex/Cor/Dictionary?id=calamitas-calamity) (critical failure).
