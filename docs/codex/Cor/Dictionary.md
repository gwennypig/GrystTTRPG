# Dictionary

## <span style="color:#ff3333">ACCRVE</span> / Accrue

During the [<span style="color:#ff3333;font-weight:600">PHASEACCRETIALIS</span>](Dictionary.md#phaseaccretialis), [<span style="color:#e2725b;font-weight:600">PERSONA</span>](Dictionary.md#persona)e with agency simultaneously gain [<span style="color:#ff3333;font-weight:600">MOMENTVM</span>](Dictionary.md#momentvm).

## <span style="color:#43b3ae">ADDENDVM</span> / Addon

An [<span style="color:#43b3ae;font-weight:600">ADDENDVM</span>](Dictionary.md#addendvm) is a gambit added during the [<span style="color:#43b3ae;font-weight:600">ADDENDVM</span>](Dictionary.md#addendvm) window. By paying the [<span style="color:#43b3ae;font-weight:600">ADDENDVM</span>](Dictionary.md#addendvm) cost of a [<span style="color:#70e697;font-weight:600">GAMBIT</span>](Dictionary.md#gambit), a [<span style="color:#e2725b;font-weight:600">PERSONA</span>](Dictionary.md#persona) may jump into the fray in support of their [<span style="color:#66023c;font-weight:600">COHORS</span>](Dictionary.md#cohors).

## <span style="color:#ff0000">CALAMITAS</span> / Calamity

Your [<span style="color:#70e697;font-weight:600">GAMBIT</span>](Dictionary.md#gambit) hasn't just [<span style="color:#8c0303;font-weight:600">CLADES</span>](Dictionary.md#clades)'d. It has [<span style="color:#ff0000;font-weight:600">CALAMITAS</span>](Dictionary.md#calamitas)'d. You or your [<span style="color:#66023c;font-weight:600">COHORS</span>](Dictionary.md#cohors) are worse than when you started.

## <span style="color:#ffd500">CARDINALIS</span> / Pivotal

The [<span style="color:#ffd500;font-weight:600">CARDINALIS</span>](Dictionary.md#cardinalis) character sets the scene. They have access to [<span style="color:#0004ff;font-weight:600">[CARDINALIS]</span>](Tags.md#cardinalis) [<span style="color:#70e697;font-weight:600">GAMBIT</span>](Dictionary.md#gambit)s and initialize the [<span style="color:#708090;font-weight:600">TABVLA</span>](Dictionary.md#tabvla) upon selecting a [<span style="color:#70e697;font-weight:600">GAMBIT</span>](Dictionary.md#gambit).

## <span style="color:#9d8015">CHORAGVS</span> / DM

The Dungeon Master. During Roman times, the [<span style="color:#9d8015;font-weight:600">CHORAGVS</span>](Dictionary.md#choragvs) was the patron of the Roman art of theatre. This person, known by a Greek-derived title, oversaw the entire logistics and production of the [<span style="color:#dc143c;font-weight:600">FABVLA</span>](Dictionary.md#fabvla).

## <span style="color:#000000">CHORAGVS PERSONAEQVE GRYSTI</span> / CPQG

The [<span style="color:#9d8015;font-weight:600">CHORAGVS</span>](Dictionary.md#choragvs) and [<span style="color:#e2725b;font-weight:600">PERSONA</span>](Dictionary.md#persona)e of Gryst.
Search for "SPQR" if you are not familiar with the term. This is the table and all concerned parties.

## <span style="color:#8c0303">CLADES</span> / Failure

You have failed in your [<span style="color:#70e697;font-weight:600">GAMBIT</span>](Dictionary.md#gambit).

## <span style="color:#ff0000">ClCa</span>

If something is conditional on [<span style="color:#ff0000;font-weight:600">ClCa</span>](Dictionary.md#clca), it is conditional on either [<span style="color:#8c0303;font-weight:600">CLADES</span>](Dictionary.md#clades) or [<span style="color:#ff0000;font-weight:600">CALAMITAS</span>](Dictionary.md#calamitas).

## <span style="color:#66023c">COHORS</span> / Cohort

Any group of [<span style="color:#e2725b;font-weight:600">PERSONA</span>](Dictionary.md#persona)e with [<span style="color:#66023c;font-weight:600">CONCORDIA</span>](Dictionary.md#concordia) amongst themselves become a [<span style="color:#66023c;font-weight:600">COHORS</span>](Dictionary.md#cohors).
This is the "party" in Gryst. [<span style="color:#e2725b;font-weight:600">PERSONA</span>](Dictionary.md#persona)e within a [<span style="color:#66023c;font-weight:600">COHORS</span>](Dictionary.md#cohors) may freely contribute during the [<span style="color:#43b3ae;font-weight:600">ADDENDVM</span>](Dictionary.md#addendvm) window without needing [<span style="color:#9d64f2;font-weight:600">[INTERVENTVS]</span>](Tags.md#interventvs) or an [<span style="color:#9d64f2;font-weight:600">OCCĀSIŌTVRBIDA</span>](Dictionary.md#occ-si-tvrbida).

## <span style="color:#66023c">CONCORDIA</span> / Cooperation

[<span style="color:#e2725b;font-weight:600">PERSONA</span>](Dictionary.md#persona)e with [<span style="color:#66023c;font-weight:600">CONCORDIA</span>](Dictionary.md#concordia) may freely contribute during the [<span style="color:#43b3ae;font-weight:600">ADDENDVM</span>](Dictionary.md#addendvm) window without needing [<span style="color:#9d64f2;font-weight:600">[INTERVENTVS]</span>](Tags.md#interventvs) or an [<span style="color:#9d64f2;font-weight:600">OCCĀSIŌTVRBIDA</span>](Dictionary.md#occ-si-tvrbida).

When a player is told to [<span style="color:#e770ff;font-weight:600">DESIGNA</span>](Dictionary.md#designa)te, they are making a choice, such as what a [<span style="color:#70e697;font-weight:600">GAMBIT</span>](Dictionary.md#gambit), is "targeting".

## <span style="color:#dc143c">FABVLA</span> / Campaign

The story that the [<span style="color:#000000;font-weight:600">CHORAGVS PERSONAEQVE GRYSTI</span>](Dictionary.md#choragvs-personaeqve-grysti) is trying to tell. It is the campaign that we have embarked on. The story waiting to unfold.

## <span style="color:#70e697">GAMBIT</span>

A [<span style="color:#70e697;font-weight:600">GAMBIT</span>](Dictionary.md#gambit) is the atomic unit of resolution. Any action that requires mechanical resolution is a [<span style="color:#70e697;font-weight:600">GAMBIT</span>](Dictionary.md#gambit). Most [<span style="color:#70e697;font-weight:600">GAMBIT</span>](Dictionary.md#gambit)s can have 1 of 4 outcomes; [<span style="color:#00ff7b;font-weight:600">SVCCVSSVS</span>](Dictionary.md#svccvssvs) (success), [<span style="color:#8c0303;font-weight:600">CLADES</span>](Dictionary.md#clades) (failure), [<span style="color:#ffea00;font-weight:600">TRIVMPHVS</span>](Dictionary.md#trivmphvs) (critical success), or [<span style="color:#ff0000;font-weight:600">CALAMITAS</span>](Dictionary.md#calamitas) (critical failure).
