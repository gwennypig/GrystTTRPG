# Tags

Tags define what something IS and what it CAN DO. They appear on Gambits, Modules, and Loculi.

---

## Modifier

### <span style="color:#9d64f2">[INTERVENTVS]</span> / Intervention

A [<span style="color:#70e697;font-weight:600">GAMBIT</span>](/codex/Cor/Dictionary.md#gambit) tagged with [<span style="color:#9d64f2;font-weight:600">[INTERVENTVS]</span>](/codex/Cor/Tags.md#interventvs-intervention) allows it to be chosen when you would have [<span style="color:#ff3333;font-weight:600">MOMENTVM</span>](/codex/Cor/Dictionary.md#momentvm-mtm) priority during the [<span style="color:#708090;font-weight:600">TABVLA</span>](/codex/Cor/Dictionary.md#tabvla-queue) even if you should have been passed over due to not having [<span style="color:#66023c;font-weight:600">CONCORDIA</span>](/codex/Cor/Dictionary.md#concordia-cooperation) with the [<span style="color:#66023c;font-weight:600">COHORS</span>](/codex/Cor/Dictionary.md#cohors-cohort) [<span style="color:#ffd500;font-weight:600">CARDINALIS</span>](/codex/Cor/Dictionary.md#cardinalis-pivotal).
Many [<span style="color:#70e697;font-weight:600">GAMBIT</span>](/codex/Cor/Dictionary.md#gambit)s tagged with [<span style="color:#9d64f2;font-weight:600">[INTERVENTVS]</span>](/codex/Cor/Tags.md#interventvs-intervention) also create an [<span style="color:#9d64f2;font-weight:600">OCCĀSIŌTVRBIDA</span>](/codex/Cor/Dictionary.md#occ-si-tvrbida-chaotic-moment).

### <span style="color:#00ff11">[INFALLIBILIS]</span> / Infallible

An [<span style="color:#00ff11;font-weight:600">[INFALLIBILIS]</span>](/codex/Cor/Tags.md#infallibilis-infallible) [<span style="color:#70e697;font-weight:600">GAMBIT</span>](/codex/Cor/Dictionary.md#gambit) cannot [<span style="color:#ff0000;font-weight:600">ClCa</span>](/codex/Cor/Dictionary.md#clca) (fail or critical fail). It can still [<span style="color:#ffea00;font-weight:600">TRIVMPHVS</span>](/codex/Cor/Dictionary.md#trivmphvs-triumph), as opposed to [<span style="color:#000000;font-weight:600">[ABSOLUTUM]</span>](/codex/Cor/Tags.md#absolutum-absolute), which can only [<span style="color:#00ff7b;font-weight:600">SVCCVSSVS</span>](/codex/Cor/Dictionary.md#svccvssvs-success).

### <span style="color:#30fd89">[SIMPLEX]</span> / Simple

 [<span style="color:#30fd89;font-weight:600">[SIMPLEX]</span>](/codex/Cor/Tags.md#simplex-simple) denotes the tagged as a simpler concept. For example, [<span style="color:#70e697;font-weight:600">GAMBIT</span>](/codex/Cor/Dictionary.md#gambit)s that are simple don't require as much mental stack. [<span style="color:#70e697;font-weight:600">GAMBIT</span>](/codex/Cor/Dictionary.md#gambit)s that are defined as [<span style="color:#30fd89;font-weight:600">[SIMPLEX]</span>](/codex/Cor/Tags.md#simplex-simple) benefit from many boons that support [<span style="color:#30fd89;font-weight:600">[SIMPLEX]</span>](/codex/Cor/Tags.md#simplex-simple) [<span style="color:#70e697;font-weight:600">GAMBIT</span>](/codex/Cor/Dictionary.md#gambit)s.

### <span style="color:#000000">[ABSOLUTUM]</span> / Absolute

This [<span style="color:#70e697;font-weight:600">GAMBIT</span>](/codex/Cor/Dictionary.md#gambit) does not require a check. It always results in [<span style="color:#00ff7b;font-weight:600">SVCCVSSVS</span>](/codex/Cor/Dictionary.md#svccvssvs-success).

### <span style="color:#0004ff">[CARDINALIS]</span> / Pivotal

A [<span style="color:#70e697;font-weight:600">GAMBIT</span>](/codex/Cor/Dictionary.md#gambit) [<span style="color:#0004ff;font-weight:600">[CARDINALIS]</span>](/codex/Cor/Tags.md#cardinalis-pivotal) cannot be chosen as an [<span style="color:#43b3ae;font-weight:600">ADDENDVM</span>](/codex/Cor/Dictionary.md#addendvm-addon).

## Form

### <span style="color:#a600ff">[MACHINA]</span> / Walker

[<span style="color:#a600ff;font-weight:600">[MACHINA]</span>](/codex/Cor/Tags.md#machina-walker)e or Walkers are the tanks of Gryst. They are mechanical vehicles, often crafted in the image of Grystborn.

### <span style="color:#ee5858">[TELUM]</span> / Missile

Ranged armament, whether that be a plasma rifle, old timey six shooter, A [<span style="color:#a600ff;font-weight:600">[MACHINA]</span>](/codex/Cor/Tags.md#machina-walker)'s shoulder missiles, a javelin, a bow, et cetera.
