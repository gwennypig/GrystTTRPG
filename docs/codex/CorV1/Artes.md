# Artēs

Artēs (Skills) are bundles of Gambits and Virtutes that characters can learn. Each ability within an Ars has a Peritia (proficiency) requirement.

---

## <span style="color:#ff9500">Ars Armignifera</span> / Fire-armament Arts

> Sub-art of **Ars Telaria**

A sub-art of [<span style="color:#bdbdbd;font-weight:600">Ars Telaria</span>](/codex/CorV1/Artes?id=ars-telaria-projectile-arts). Concerns the skill of operating [<span style="color:#ff881a;font-weight:600">[ARMA SULPHURICA]</span>](/codex/CorV1/Tags?id=arma-sulphurica-conventional-firearms).
