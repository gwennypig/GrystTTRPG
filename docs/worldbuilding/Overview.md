# Worldbuilding Overview

#ttrpg #worldbuilding #lore

Gryst is a story set in a world fundamentally unlike our own — a reality where the very substance of existence can be seen, touched, and shaped by those attuned to it.

---

## The Grystborn

The dominant sentient lifeform of this world is a people known by many names, both among themselves and by outsiders. The most common name is **Grystborn**.

Most Grystborn are uniquely attuned to Gryst — able to feel its presence in the world, sense its currents, and manipulate it through will, craft, or devotion. This attunement is what defines them as a people, though the degree and nature of that connection varies wildly between individuals.

---

## Gryst: The Elementary Principle

**Gryst** is the fundamental substance of this world. Everything that exists can be broken down into Gryst. It is the elementary principle — the building block of reality itself.

In its most common state, Gryst appears as an **inert colored dust**. Each color corresponds to a primal element, and the combination of different Gryst types gives rise to all matter, energy, and life.

---

## The Six Grysts

### Blazing Gryst
**Color:** Red  
**Primal State:** Fire

Blazing Gryst burns. In its inert form, it appears as rust-red dust, warm to the touch. When awakened, it becomes flame — from the spark of a candle to the inferno of a volcano.

---

### Flowing Gryst
**Color:** Blue  
**Primal State:** Water

Flowing Gryst moves. Its inert dust has a cool, slick quality, almost damp even when dry. When awakened, it becomes water in all its forms — rivers, rain, mist, ice.

---

### Storming Gryst
**Color:** Purple  
**Primal State:** Storm (Lightning, Thunder)

Storming Gryst crackles with potential. Even inert, it carries a faint static charge. When awakened, it becomes the storm — lightning that splits the sky, thunder that shakes the earth, winds that howl with fury.

---

### Blooming Gryst
**Color:** Green  
**Primal State:** Earth and Life

Blooming Gryst grows. Its inert dust smells of soil after rain, of moss and growing things. When awakened, it becomes the earth itself — stone and soil — and from it, life springs forth. Plants, fungi, the vital force that animates living things.

---

### Radiant Gryst
**Color:** Brilliant (shimmering, luminous)  
**Primal State:** Light

Radiant Gryst shines. Even in its inert state, it glimmers and catches the eye, never truly dull. When awakened, it becomes light — illumination, warmth without heat, the brilliance of the sun and stars.

---

### Murky Gryst
**Color:** Devoid of light (unnaturally dark)  
**Primal State:** The Nothing

Murky Gryst *takes*. Even inert, it appears unnaturally dark, seeming to draw light away from its surroundings. When awakened, it becomes absence — shadow, void, the nothing between things. It is not merely darkness, but the *lack* of what should be there.

---

## The Composition of Reality

Everything in the world of Gryst is composed of combinations of these six types:

- A **flame** is nearly pure Blazing Gryst
- A **tree** is mostly Blooming Gryst, with traces of Flowing and Radiant
- A **storm cloud** is Storming and Flowing intertwined
- A **shadow** is Murky Gryst pooling where Radiant cannot reach
- A **living creature** is a complex weave of all six, in ratios unique to each species

The Grystborn, attuned to this fundamental truth, can perceive these compositions — and those with sufficient gnosis can manipulate them.

## Implications

### For Sorcerers
Those who understand Gryst intuitively can awaken the dust with a thought, shaping fire from Blazing Gryst or calling lightning from Storming. Their gnosis is felt, not learned.

### For Engineers
Those who understand Gryst systematically can build devices that channel and combine Gryst types — forges that burn hotter by concentrating Blazing, or cooling systems that leverage Flowing.

### For Everyone
Even those without special attunement live in a world *made* of Gryst. The food they eat, the air they breathe, the ground beneath their feet — all Gryst, in endless combination.

---

## Design Notes

The six-element system provides:
- **Visual identity** — Each Gryst type has a distinct color
- **Intuitive associations** — Fire, water, storm, earth, light, void
- **Combination potential** — Mix elements for complex effects
- **Aesthetic hooks** — Characters can favor certain Gryst types

The Murky Gryst ("the nothing") intentionally sits opposite Radiant, creating a light/void duality that can drive conflict and mystery.
