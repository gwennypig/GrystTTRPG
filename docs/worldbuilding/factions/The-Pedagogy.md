# The Pedagogy

#ttrpg #worldbuilding #faction #pedagogy #flowing

> *"Knowledge, like water, seeks its level. It cannot be dammed forever. It will spread."*

## Overview

The **Pedagogy** is an ancient cult of researchers and teachers — fervent believers in the preservation and spread of knowledge. Centered on **Flowing Gryst**, they see water as the perfect metaphor: just as hydrostatics causes water to level out, so too must knowledge spread equally to all.

They are older than the Forgelords. Older than the Consensus. They are not certain of their own origins.

| Aspect | Detail |
|--------|--------|
| **Primary Gryst** | Flowing (Blue/Water) |
| **Structure** | Decentralized; no ranks; meetings of the Wise |
| **Purpose** | Preserve and spread all knowledge |
| **Titles** | Scholar (all), Wise (school founders) |
| **Age** | Ancient; predates Consensus and Forgelords |

## Philosophy: The Leveling of Knowledge

The Pedagogy believes that knowledge **must never be hoarded**. Like water finding its level, information must flow outward — from those who have it to those who need it.

This is not mere ideology. It is religious conviction:

- **Water is unstoppable** — Dams break. Rivers find new paths. So too does truth.
- **Hoarding is sin** — To withhold knowledge is to fight the nature of reality.
- **Teaching is sacred** — Every Scholar is obligated to share what they know.

## Structure: Radical Egalitarianism

### No Ranks

There are **no ranks** in the Pedagogy, for knowledge belongs to all equally. Every member is addressed simply as **Scholar** — whether they joined yesterday or have studied for centuries.

This is not mere humility. It is doctrine.

### The One Exception: The Wise

The **only exception** is a Scholar who founds their own school. These individuals are addressed as **Wise** — not because they know more, but because they have created a place where knowledge can spread.

### Governance (Or Lack Thereof)

The only form of government is a **meeting of Wise Scholars**. There is no official mechanism for this. No schedules. No voting procedures. No binding resolutions.

It is simply a **culture** — Scholars teach, research, communicate, share. The Wise occasionally gather when matters require discussion. Consensus emerges organically or not at all.

## Presence: Everywhere Knowledge Is Wanted

The Pedagogy is **deeply entrenched in all sorts of societies**. They work with anyone who wants them:

- The **Consensus** — Training bureaucrats, preserving records
- The **Forgelords** — Teaching craft techniques, engineering knowledge
- Independent worlds, tribal societies, spacefaring empires

They are not aligned with any faction. They are aligned with **knowledge itself**.

Where there is a civilization that values learning, there is likely a Pedagogy school. Where there is research to be done, there are likely Pedagogy scholars. They flow into every space that will accept them.

---

## The Unknown Origins

Here is the paradox: **the Pedagogy is not certain of its own origin**.

They are much older than the Forgelords. Much older than the Consensus. Their records stretch back further than most civilizations' memories. But somewhere in that deep past, the trail goes cold.

For an organization dedicated to the preservation of knowledge, this gap is... uncomfortable. Some Scholars spend their lives trying to fill it.

---

## Religion: Knowledge Unworshipped

The Pedagogy **has a religion**. It is polytheistic. It involves gods.

But they **do not practice or worship it**.

Instead, they simply **know of its existence** — and study it as a relic of their past, no different from any other subject. The old gods of the Pedagogy are data points. Their myths are historical documents. Their rituals are anthropological curiosities.

> "We do not worship. We catalog."

The religion of the Pedagogy is just **knowledge, no strings attached**.

## The Living God

And yet.

There exists a **living god** that has access to all the knowledge gathered by the Pedagogy. This entity leads the Pedagogy in the analysis of data — guiding research, connecting disparate findings, seeing patterns no mortal mind could perceive.

### The Question

Whether this god:
- **IS** gnosis
- **GOVERNS** gnosis
- Is **CREATED BY** gnosis
- Is simply **ALIGNED WITH** gnosis

...is unknown.

Perhaps it is **unknowable**.

Perhaps it is **undefined**.

```
┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ THE PEDAGOGY'S GOD                                 │
│                                                    │
│  Access: All knowledge the Pedagogy has gathered   │
│  Role: Guides analysis, reveals patterns           │
│  Nature: Unknown                                   │
│                                                    │

│  Was it created by Gnosis? Aligned with it?        │
│                                                    │
│  Perhaps unknowable.                               │

```

This entity is an example of something at the very **limits of Gnosis** — currently outside the realm of understanding, perhaps even by other deities. It embodies the very principle the Pedagogy pursues: knowledge that recedes even as you reach for it.

## Story Hooks

- **The Origin Hunt** — A Scholar becomes obsessed with finding the Pedagogy's lost history. What did they forget? Why?
- **Forbidden Knowledge** — Some things should not be spread. What does the Pedagogy do when knowledge itself is dangerous?
- **The Wise Gathering** — A meeting of the Wise is called. Something is wrong. What could unite such individualists?
- **The Living God Speaks** — The entity reveals something. A pattern in the data. A warning. A question.
- **The School in the War Zone** — A Pedagogy school stands between two armies. The Scholars refuse to leave. Knowledge must be preserved.

## Design Notes

The Pedagogy provides:

- **Neutral faction** — Works with everyone, aligned with no one
- **Information brokers** — Need to know something? Find a Scholar
- **Ancient mystery** — What is their origin? What is their god?
- **Philosophical foil** — They contrast with hoarders, secret-keepers, authoritarian states
- **Decentralized model** — Shows that structure isn't always hierarchy
- **The Undefined God** — A deliberate connection to the RDC principle; some things remain unspecified until they must be defined
