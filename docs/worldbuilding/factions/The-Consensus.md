# The Consensus (Shattered)

#ttrpg #worldbuilding #faction #consensus #history

> *"For a thousand years, the Consensus was the universe. Now it is memory."*

## Overview

The **Consensus** was the dominant galactic power that united most of the known universe under a single political framework. For generations, to be "in Consensus" meant to be civilized, protected, and connected to the great web of interstellar commerce and law.

**The Consensus has shattered.**

What remains are fragments — successor states, breakaway factions, former allies now adrift, and the echoes of institutions that once governed billions.

## What the Consensus Was

### Universal Framework

The Consensus was not an empire in the traditional sense. It was a **framework for cooperation** — a vast agreement among civilizations to abide by common laws, trade in common currency, and resolve disputes through common institutions.

Membership was technically voluntary. In practice, being outside the Consensus meant being outside civilization itself.

### The Cryst Currency

The Consensus established the **universal Cryst currency** — a standardized medium of exchange accepted across all member worlds. This economic unification was perhaps its greatest achievement.

### Bureaucracy as Technology

The Consensus pioneered and spread the **"science" of bureaucracy** — systematic administration that allowed coordination across impossible distances. Many factions adopted it. Few loved it.

### Laws and Norms

The Consensus established legal norms including:
- **Prohibition of slavery** (de jure, if not always de facto)
- **Protection of trade routes**
- **Arbitration of disputes**
- **Standards for technology and safety**

## The Shattering

*[Details of the Shattering to be developed]*

What caused the Consensus to collapse is a matter of ongoing debate, propaganda, and historical revision. Likely factors:

- Internal contradictions (like tolerance of Forgelord servitude)
- Overextension
- Ideological fractures
- External threats
- Economic strain
- Loss of legitimacy

The result: a universe without its center. Former member states now navigate a fractured political landscape.

## Story Implications

### The Post-Consensus Era

Campaigns set in the current era deal with the **aftermath** of the Shattering:

- Former allies become rivals or enemies
- Institutions lose legitimacy
- Currency may or may not hold value
- Laws exist but enforcement is fragmented
- Power vacuums invite opportunists

### Consensus Loyalists

Some factions still believe in the Consensus ideal and work toward reunification — or a successor state that embodies its principles.

### Consensus Rejecters

Others view the Shattering as liberation. The Consensus was always flawed, always oppressive in subtle ways. Good riddance.

### The Pragmatists

Most simply deal with the world as it is. The Consensus is gone. What matters is what comes next.

## Design Notes

The Consensus provides:
- **Fallen empire backstory** — Explains why there's shared infrastructure but no central power
- **Moral complexity** — Was it good? Bad? Necessary? Oppressive? Yes.
- **Campaign flexibility** — Set games during Consensus, during Shattering, or after
- **Faction motivations** — Everyone has opinions about what the Consensus was and should have been
- **Mystery hooks** — What *really* caused the Shattering?
