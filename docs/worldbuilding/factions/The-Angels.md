# The Angels

#ttrpg #worldbuilding #faction #angels #historical #mystery

> *"They walked the heavens before any of us knew how to look up."*

## Overview

The **Angels** are a lost civilization — the earliest known spacefaring people. Almost nothing is known about them except that they existed, they traveled between stars, and they left behind relics that taught others to follow.

| Aspect | Detail |
|--------|--------|
| **Status** | Lost / Extinct (presumed) |
| **Origin** | Unknown |
| **Species** | Thought to be Grystborn, but unconfirmed |
| **Legacy** | Relics that enabled modern spacefaring |
| **Language** | Undeciphered |

---

## What We Know

Almost nothing.

The Angels were **the earliest known spacefaring civilization**. Their relics have been found across vast distances, suggesting they traveled between stars when no one else could.

They are **thought to be Grystborn**, but this is uncertain. Their remains (if any exist) have not been conclusively identified. Their language has not been deciphered despite centuries of Pedagogy effort.

---

## The Name

Why "Angels"?

Space is commonly called **the Heavens** in Grystborn cosmology. Beings who walked the heavens before anyone else? The Pedagogy, with their characteristic dry humor, named them Angels.

The name stuck.

---

## Legacy: The Relics

The Angels left behind **relics** — artifacts, installations, perhaps even derelict vessels. These relics contained knowledge (or could be reverse-engineered) that taught other civilizations the secrets of spacefaring.

**Who learned from them:**
- The Pedagogy
- The Consensus
- Countless others

Most modern spacefaring civilizations owe their ability to reach the stars to Angel relics, directly or indirectly.

---

## The Pedagogy Theory

The Pedagogy has a theory about their own forgotten religion:

> "The gods we once worshipped were merely Angels — beings who visited our homeworld in the ancient past and were mistaken for divine."

If true, this would mean the Pedagogy's polytheistic religion (which they now study but do not worship) is simply a mythologized memory of first contact.

---

## Mysteries

- **What were they?** Grystborn? Something else?
- **Where did they come from?** No homeworld has been identified.
- **Where did they go?** Extinct? Transcended? Hiding?
- **What did they want?** The relics seem intentional, but their purpose is unclear.
- **Can their language be deciphered?** The Pedagogy keeps trying.

---

## Design Notes

The Angels provide:
- **Ancient mystery** — The "precursor civilization" trope
- **Reason for spacefaring** — Explains how multiple civilizations independently reached the stars
- **Campaign hooks** — Angel relics, undeciphered texts, lost tombs
- **Theological implications** — Were gods just misremembered aliens?
- **Blank canvas** — Deliberately undefined; GM can reveal or preserve mystery
