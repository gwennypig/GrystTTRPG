# The Quarum

#ttrpg #worldbuilding #faction #quarum #blooming #construct #mystery

> *"They do not know why they exist. Neither do we."*

## Overview

The **Quarum** are a sentient tribe of seemingly golden constructs — mechanical beings of unknown origin, unknown purpose, and unknown homeworld. They are **not Grystborn**. They are composed entirely of **Blooming Gryst** (the earthen essence), and they have existed in sparse isolation for as long as anyone can remember.

| Aspect | Detail |
|--------|--------|
| **Species** | Constructs (not Grystborn) |
| **Composition** | Entirely Blooming Gryst (golden appearance) |
| **Homeworld** | Unknown |
| **Status** | Sparse; mostly dormant |
| **Origin** | Unknown (attributed to Angels) |

---

## Appearance

The Quarum appear as **golden constructs** — mechanical in nature, with forms that suggest intentional design. They gleam with the warm tones of awakened Blooming Gryst, though their construction is unlike any known Grystborn craft.

They are not organic. They are not machines in the conventional sense. They are something else.

---

## Distribution

The Quarum are found in only **a few worlds** in a lesser-explored star system. Their distribution is sparse — isolated individuals or small clusters, rarely more.

No homeworld has been identified. Either they scattered from somewhere long forgotten, or they were placed deliberately in their current locations.

---

## Dormancy and Activation

Most Quarum encountered are **deactivated and dormant** — inert golden statues that might be mistaken for art or religious icons.

However, they can be **energized with modern technology**. Supply them with power, and they awaken.

### Adaptive Technology

The Quarum possess **adaptive technology compatibility**. When activated, they can integrate external technology into themselves:

- Power sources become part of their system
- Communication devices grant them speech synthesis
- Sensors extend their perception

This integration is seamless and unsettling. The technology becomes *them*.

---

## Gryst Attunement

The Quarum have a **weaker connection to Gryst** than Grystborn. They are not as attuned to the six elements as organics are.

However, they can **manipulate their own Blooming Gryst essence** more freely than the average Grystborn can manipulate any Gryst. Their self-modification is intuitive where their external manipulation is limited.

| Capability | Quarum | Grystborn |
|------------|--------|-----------|
| General Gryst attunement | Weak | Strong |
| Self-Gryst manipulation | Strong | Limited |
| External Gryst manipulation | Limited | Variable |

---

## Origin: Unknown

The Quarum **do not know their purpose or origin**.

They are awake. They are aware. They can think, communicate, and act. But they do not know why they exist, who made them, or what they were meant to do.

Like so many unknowns in the universe, those with gnosis of the Quarum **chalk it up to the Angels**. It's as good a theory as any.

---

## Story Hooks

- **Awakening** — A Quarum activates. What does it want? What does it remember?
- **The Scattered Pattern** — The Quarum placements seem intentional. Is there a message in their distribution?
- **Integration** — A Quarum integrates dangerous technology. What happens when constructs adapt weapons?
- **The Purpose** — A Quarum claims to remember why they exist. Is it true? Is it fabricated?
- **Angel Connection** — Evidence emerges linking the Quarum to Angel relics. What were they for?

---

## Design Notes

The Quarum provide:
- **Non-Grystborn species** — Different perspective on the setting
- **Living artifacts** — Awakening dormant constructs is a natural adventure hook
- **Adaptive tech** — Interesting implications for equipment and modification
- **Angel mystery** — Another thread connecting to the precursor civilization
- **Existential questions** — Sapient beings who don't know why they exist
