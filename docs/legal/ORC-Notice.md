## Licensed Material

The following game mechanics and systems are **Licensed Material** under the ORC License. You may use, modify, and build upon these freely:

### Core Resolution System
- The two-axis resolution concept (fate dice for success/failure, magnitude dice for effect scaling)
- The four-tier outcome structure (critical success, success, failure, critical failure)
- The concept of dice-agnostic fate resolution with DM-selected dice
- The concept of a fixed die (d20) for magnitude/physics calculations

### Gambit System
- The Gambit as an atomic unit of resolution containing action, outcomes, and formulas
- The three-layer Gambit structure (pre-authored, GM-modified, improvised)
- Module-granted Gambits as a design pattern
- Core/fallback Gambits as universal actions
- The outcome-as-interface design philosophy

### Momentum System
- Simultaneous momentum accumulation during play
- Threshold-based turn triggering
- Momentum as a physical prop (d20 tracking)
- Stack-based action resolution
- Coupling/hijacking mechanics for action chaining

### Module System
- The three-stat module design (Mass/capacity, Complexitās/cost, Magnitūdō/scaling)
- Loculi as module slots with flexible placement
- Tags as skill-linking descriptors
- Required Disambiguation Canonization (undefined details becoming canon when relevant)

### Party & Cooperation
- Party-based action economy
- Shared momentum pools
- Cooperative action chaining

### Design Principles
- "Magic equals technology" unified aesthetic system
- GM-empowered outcome flexibility with sacred outcome quality
- Negotiated probability, authored outcomes
- The "good roll always good, bad roll always bad" principle
