## What Is Gryst?

**Gryst; ESORPG** — an Esoteric System-Oriented Roleplaying Game.

This is not another fantasy heartbreaker. This is not a D&D clone with different dice. This is an attempt to solve a problem that most TTRPGs don't acknowledge: **the campaign that never ends.**

---

## The Problem

Traditional TTRPGs are built on **vertical progression**. Characters level up. Numbers get bigger. Challenges scale. Eventually, the campaign ends — either because the story concludes, or because the system buckles under its own weight.

What happens when:

- A new player wants to join mid-campaign?
- A veteran player can't make it for six months?
- The group splinters into two, then rejoins?
- You want to keep playing these characters *forever*?

Most systems shrug. "Start over. Roll new characters. Begin again."

**Gryst refuses.**

---

## The Vision

Gryst is built for **horizontal progression** — not climbing a ladder, but spreading roots.

### Play Forever

Your group should be able to play their campaign indefinitely. Not because nothing changes, but because change doesn't break the system. Characters grow *outward* — gaining new capabilities, new stories, new connections — without obsoleting what came before.

### Join Anytime

New players should be able to drop in without feeling like second-class citizens. A character created today should be able to stand beside a character who's been played for three years — different, not lesser.

### Split and Merge

Groups should be able to fracture and reform. Run a side campaign with half the party. Bring them back. Cross over with another table. The system accommodates.

### Modularity as Philosophy

Everything in Gryst is **modular**. Not just mechanically — philosophically. Loculi hold Modules. Modules grant Gambits. Content packs extend the world. Nothing is welded in place. Everything can be swapped, replaced, combined.

---

## The Esoteric System

To achieve this, Gryst embraces abstraction. It doesn't simulate physics — it simulates *narrative structure*. The rules are not a physics engine; they're a grammar for collaborative storytelling.

This makes Gryst:

- **Abstract** — mechanisms represent narrative beats, not physical causation
- **Modular** — every piece is designed to be combined with others
- **Esoteric** — the system has its own internal logic, its own language, its own philosophy

The Latin terminology isn't pretension — it's precision. When everyone shares a vocabulary, the improvisation has structure. When the structure is abstract, it bends without breaking.

---

## The Attempt

This is my unique attempt at solving a problem I created in my head.

Maybe no one else has this problem. Maybe every group is content with campaigns that end, with characters that retire, with stories that conclude.

But I wanted a system that could hold a story forever. That could grow with its players. That treated the game itself as a living thing — something you tend, not something you complete.

Gryst is that attempt.

---

## Core Principles

### 1. Horizontal Over Vertical

Characters expand their capabilities laterally. More options, not bigger numbers. A veteran character has *breadth*, not just *height*.

### 2. Everything Is A Module

If it can be swapped out, it should be. If it can be extended, it should be. Rigidity is the enemy of longevity.

### 3. Magic Is Tech, Tech Is Magic

There is no distinction. Gryst is the fabric of reality — sorcerers feel it viscerally, engineers understand it systematically. Both manipulate the same truth through different gnosis.

### 4. The System Serves The Table

Rules exist to enable play, not constrain it. When a rule gets in the way of the story, the story wins. The system is a servant, not a master.

### 5. Embrace The Esoteric

Gryst has its own language, its own concepts, its own way of thinking. Learning that language is part of the experience. The system rewards those who engage with its internal logic.
