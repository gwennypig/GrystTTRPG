## The Elevator Pitch
Gryst, The Tabletop Roleplaying Game is a spacefantasy adventure set in the universe of Gryst. Grystborn come in all shapes and sizes, once unified under a bureaucratic empire now shattered. The Novus Consensus strives to maintain the old way, radical acceptance and coexistence, rule by a bureaucratic elite class built upon dreams of meritocracy and equality. Grystborn technology conquered all the heavens, infinite worlds connected by heavenstriders, titanic walkers roamed the lands, and brave heroes made their mark on history. In the Gryst TTRPG, you assume the role of a Grystborn, Gryst is an elemental particle of which the fabric of physical reality is composed of. Grystborn are not born of previous Grystborn, instead spawning from worlds themselves out of pure energy.
