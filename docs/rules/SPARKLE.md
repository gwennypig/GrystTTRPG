# SPARKLE Attributes

#ttrpg #rules #sparkle #attributes

## Overview

**SPARKLE** is the acronym for Gryst's seven core attributes:

| Letter | Attribute | Description |
|--------|-----------|-------------|
| **S** | Strength | Physical power, lifting, melee impact, carrying capacity |
| **P** | Perception | Awareness, accuracy, noticing details, reading situations |
| **A** | Agility | Speed, reflexes, coordination, **determines Momentum Gain** |
| **R** | Rizz | Social grace, charm, negotiation, performance, intimidation |
| **K** | Knowledge | Education, memory, technical skill, problem-solving |
| **L** | Luck | Fate's favor, critical chances, "things just work out" |
| **E** | Endurance | Health, stamina, resistance to harm, staying power |

---

## SubSPARKLES

Each SPARKLE can be specialized into **SubSPARKLES** — narrower focuses within the broader attribute.

### How It Works

When you gain a SPARKLE point, you choose:

1. **Generalized** — adds to the base SPARKLE, applies everywhere
2. **Specialized** — assign to a SubSPARKLE, higher ceiling for specific applications

**Your total SPARKLE = Generalized + Sum of SubSPARKLES**

### Example

```
Knowledge: 5 total
├── Generalized: 2
├── Arcane: 2
└── Systematics: 1
```

- When rolling general Knowledge checks: use 5
- When rolling specifically Arcane Knowledge: use 5 (but some abilities might require Arcane specifically)
- An ability requiring "Knowledge 4, Arcane 2" — you qualify!
- An ability requiring "Knowledge 4, Systematics 2" — you don't (only have 1)

## Using SPARKLE

### Checks

When the GM calls for a check, they'll specify:
- The SPARKLE attribute (and possibly a SubSPARKLE)
- The target number or opposing roll

> "Roll Perception." — Use your total Perception  
> "Roll Perception (Visual)." — Use total, but Visual-specific bonuses may apply  
> "This ability requires Perception 3, Intuition 2." — Must meet both

### Modifying Magnitude

Some Gambits use SPARKLE to modify the **Icosaedrum Magnitudinis** (d20 roll):

> "Magnitūdō = d20 + STR modifier"

The modifier is typically derived from the SPARKLE score (exact formula TBD).

### Derived Stats

Several stats derive from SPARKLE:

| Derived Stat | Primary SPARKLE |
|--------------|-----------------|
| Momentum Gain | Agility |
| Health | Endurance |
| Carrying Capacity | Strength |
| Initiative | Agility (Reflexes) |

## Design Notes

### Why SPARKLE?

- **Memorable acronym** — players remember their stats
- **Seven attributes** — enough granularity without overload
- **Luck as a stat** — embraces the chaos of TTRPGs
- **Rizz** — social ability deserves its own stat, not just "Charisma"
- **SubSPARKLES** — allows specialization without bloating the core list

### Generalized vs. Specialized Trade-off

- **Generalized** is safe — applies everywhere
- **Specialized** is powerful — unlocks specific abilities, higher peaks
- **Optimal play** is a mix — some base, some focus

### SubSPARKLES Are Soft

SubSPARKLE categories are **guidelines, not prisons**:

- A GM might allow Performance (Rizz) for a distracting dance in combat
- Arcane (Knowledge) might help identify a magical trap
- Flexibility is encouraged; rigid categorization kills creativity

## SPARKLE at Character Creation

Starting characters receive **X SPARKLE points** to distribute. Options:

### Point Buy
- Each point costs 1
- Maximum in any SPARKLE: Y (before SubSPARKLE bonuses)

### Standard Array
- Distribute: 3, 2, 2, 2, 1, 1, 0 (or similar)
- Then add SubSPARKLE picks

### Roll (Variant)
- Roll 4d6 drop lowest, seven times
- Assign as desired
- Then add SubSPARKLE picks

*Exact numbers determined by playtesting.*
