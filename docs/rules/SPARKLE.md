# SPARKLE Attributes

#ttrpg #rules #sparkle #attributes

## Overview

**SPARKLE** is the acronym for Gryst's seven core attributes:

| Letter | Attribute | Description |
|--------|-----------|-------------|
| **S** | Strength | Physical power, lifting, melee impact, carrying capacity |
| **P** | Perception | Awareness, accuracy, noticing details, reading situations |
| **A** | Agility | Speed, reflexes, coordination, **determines Momentum Gain** |
| **R** | Rizz | Social grace, charm, negotiation, performance, intimidation |
| **K** | Knowledge | Education, memory, technical skill, problem-solving |
| **L** | Luck | Fate's favor, critical chances, "things just work out" |
| **E** | Endurance | Health, stamina, resistance to harm, staying power |

---

## The Baseline

**0 SPARKLE represents the average, normalized individual.** This is the baseline — a person of typical human capability in that attribute.

- **Positive SPARKLE** — Above average, exceptional
- **Negative SPARKLE** — Below average, impaired
- **0** — Average, unremarkable

---

## SPARKLE at Character Creation

Starting characters receive **2 free points** to add to any SPARKLE attributes.

### Trading Points

You can **subtract** from any SPARKLE (down to **-5**) to gain points to add elsewhere (up to **+5** before free points).

**Example:**
```
Starting: All at 0
Subtract 3 from Strength → Strength: -3
Add those 3 to Knowledge → Knowledge: +3
Add 2 free points to Rizz → Rizz: +2

Final: S:-3 P:0 A:0 R:+2 K:+3 L:0 E:0
```

### Limits

| Minimum | Maximum |
|---------|---------|
| -5 | No hard cap (but +5 from trades + 2 free = +7 theoretical max at creation) |

## Using SPARKLE in Checks

SPARKLE determines **how many dice you roll** and whether you take the highest or lowest result.

### The Dice Pool Rule

| SPARKLE Value | Dice Rolled | Result Used |
|---------------|-------------|-------------|
| -6 or lower | 7 dice | Take **lowest** |
| -5 | 6 dice | Take **lowest** |
| -4 | 5 dice | Take **lowest** |
| -3 | 4 dice | Take **lowest** |
| -2 | 3 dice | Take **lowest** |
| -1 | 2 dice | Take **lowest** |
| **0** | **1 die** | **Baseline** |
| +1 | 2 dice | Take **highest** |
| +2 | 3 dice | Take **highest** |
| +3 | 4 dice | Take **highest** |
| +4 | 5 dice | Take **highest** |
| +5 | 6 dice | Take **highest** |
| +6 or higher | 7 dice | Take **highest** |

**In short:** Your SPARKLE value = number of *extra* dice. Positive means advantage (take highest), negative means disadvantage (take lowest).

### Combined Checks

When a check involves **multiple SPARKLE attributes**, sum them together.

> **Example:** A check requiring both Strength and Agility  
> STR: +2, AGI: -1 → Total: +1 → Roll 2 dice, take highest

### Cap

The combined total **caps at ±6** regardless of how many attributes contribute.

> STR: +4, AGI: +5 → Sum: +9 → Capped to +6 → Roll 7 dice, take highest

## SubSPARKLES

Each SPARKLE can be specialized into **SubSPARKLES** — narrower focuses within the broader attribute.

### How It Works

When you gain a SPARKLE point, you choose:

1. **Generalized** — adds to the base SPARKLE, applies everywhere
2. **Specialized** — assign to a SubSPARKLE, higher ceiling for specific applications

**Your total SPARKLE = Generalized + Relevant SubSPARKLES**

### Example

```
Knowledge: Base +2
├── Generalized: +2
├── Arcane: +2 (specialized)
└── Systematics: +1 (specialized)
```

- General Knowledge check: use +2 (generalized only)
- Arcane Knowledge check: use +4 (generalized + arcane)
- Systematics check: use +3 (generalized + systematics)
- An ability requiring "Knowledge 3, Arcane 2" — you qualify!

## Design Notes

### Why This System?

- **Intuitive baseline** — 0 is average, positive is good, negative is bad
- **Dice pool advantage/disadvantage** — more dramatic swings for extreme values
- **Combinable** — multi-attribute checks feel natural (just add them)
- **Capped** — prevents infinite stacking from breaking the game
- **Trade-offs matter** — dumping a stat has real consequences

### SubSPARKLES Are Soft

SubSPARKLE categories are **guidelines, not prisons**:

- A GM might allow Performance (Rizz) for a distracting dance in combat
- Arcane (Knowledge) might help identify a magical trap
- Flexibility is encouraged; rigid categorization kills creativity
