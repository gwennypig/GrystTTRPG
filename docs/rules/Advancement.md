# Advancement

#ttrpg #rules #progression #sparkle #skills

## Philosophy

Advancement in Gryst is about **expanding options** more than raw power. A veteran character has more Gambits, more flexibility, and deeper specialization — but a starting character is already competent and dangerous.

**Core principles:**
- **Horizontal growth** — more tools, not bigger numbers
- **Identity sharpening** — AP forces you to define who you are
- **Multiclass freedom** — branch into new Skills anytime
- **No level cap** — but diminishing returns at high levels

---

## Advancement Triggers

Characters advance through **Milestone Rewards**, granted by the GM at story beats:

| Trigger | Typical Reward |
|---------|----------------|
| Completing a major objective | SP + AP |
| Finishing a story arc | SP + AP + SPARKLE |
| Surviving a harrowing situation | AP or SPARKLE |
| Significant downtime (weeks/months) | SP |
| Exceptional roleplay moment | AP (bonus) |
| GM's discretion | Any combination |

**No XP tracking** — the GM decides when advancement happens based on narrative pacing.

## What You Gain

### Skill Points (SP)

**SP** increases your level in Skills, unlocking more Abilities.

| Spend | Effect |
|-------|--------|
| 1 SP | +1 level in a Skill you already have |
| 2 SP | +1 level in a new Skill (first level costs extra) |

*Variant: First level costs 1 SP to encourage experimentation.*

As your Skill level increases:
- **Passive Abilities** unlock automatically
- **TRAINED Abilities** become available (but still cost AP)

### Ability Points (AP)

**AP** unlocks TRAINED abilities — powerful options that require investment.

| Spend | Effect |
|-------|--------|
| 1-3 AP | Unlock one TRAINED ability (cost varies by ability) |

AP is **scarce** — you won't unlock everything. This forces identity:
- "I'm the Gunslinger who took Fan the Hammer and Deadeye"
- "I'm the Gunslinger who took Trick Shot and went into Duelist"

### SPARKLE Points

**SPARKLE** increases your raw attributes.

| Spend | Effect |
|-------|--------|
| 1 SPARKLE | +1 to a generalized SPARKLE attribute |
| 1 SPARKLE | +1 to a specific SubSPARKLE |

**Strategic choice:**
- **Generalized** — always applies, broad benefit
- **SubSPARKLE** — higher ceiling for specific applications

*Example: Knowledge 4 (Generalized 2, Arcane 1, Systematics 1)*

## Advancement Pacing

| Stage | Sessions | SP | AP | SPARKLE | What It Feels Like |
|-------|----------|----|----|---------|-------------------|
| Fresh | 0 | — | — | — | Competent adventurer, defined concept |
| Seasoned | ~5 | +3 | +2 | +1 | Specialization emerging, signature moves |
| Veteran | ~15 | +6 | +4 | +2 | Multiple skill trees, flexible toolkit |
| Elite | ~30 | +10 | +6 | +4 | Master of your domains, feared/respected |
| Legendary | ~50+ | +15 | +8 | +6 | Campaign-defining power, world-shaking |

*These are guidelines — pacing depends on campaign.*

## Advancement at the Table

### Between Sessions
The GM announces rewards at session end:

> "That was a big one — you all get **1 SP and 1 AP**. We'll do advancement at the start of next session."

### During Downtime
If the fiction has significant time skips:

> "Three months pass while you recover. Everyone gains **2 SP** from training."

### Catch-Up Mechanics
If a new player joins or someone misses sessions:

- New characters start at the **party's average** minus a small amount
- Missed sessions might grant partial SP/AP
- Or just hand-wave it — Gryst isn't a numbers race

## Skill Progression Deep Dive

As you invest SP in a Skill, you unlock tiers of Abilities:

```
SKILL LEVEL → UNLOCKS
─────────────────────────────────────
1            Tier 1 abilities (basics)
2            Tier 1 abilities
3            Tier 2 abilities (intermediate)  
4            Tier 2 abilities
5            Tier 3 abilities (advanced)
6+           Tier 3 abilities, mastery perks
```

**Passive abilities** auto-unlock. **TRAINED abilities** require AP.

### Example: Arcanist Skill

| Level | Ability | Type | TRAINED? | AP |
|-------|---------|------|----------|-----|
| 1 | Arcane Sense | Perk | No | — |
| 1 | Arcane Bolt | Gambit | No | — |
| 2 | Gryst Attunement | Perk | No | — |
| 3 | Arcane Shield | Gambit | Yes | 1 |
| 3 | Elemental Affinity | Perk | Yes | 1 |
| 5 | Reality Tear | Gambit | Yes | 2 |
| 5 | Gryst Mastery | Perk | Yes | 2 |

## Power Curve Design

### Early Game (Sessions 1-5)
- Characters have 2-3 Skills at low levels
- Core Gambits from equipment + starting abilities
- Learning the Momentum system

### Mid Game (Sessions 6-20)
- Characters have 3-5 Skills, some at moderate levels
- Signature TRAINED abilities define playstyle
- SubSPARKLE specialization matters

### Late Game (Sessions 20+)
- Characters have broad or deep skill coverage
- Multiple high-AP abilities
- SPARKLE maxing specific areas
- GM introduces world-scale challenges

### No Hard Cap
There's no "max level" — but:
- Abilities have diminishing returns at high levels
- The story should escalate to match
- Retirement is always an option (legacy characters!)

---

## Respec & Mistakes

**No full respec** — your choices matter.

**But:**
- If a player genuinely isn't enjoying their build, work with the GM
- Narrative justification can unlock changes ("You trained with the monks...")
- Early campaign (first few sessions) allows more flexibility
