# Advancement

#ttrpg #rules #progression

## Philosophy
*Why do characters advance? What does advancement MEAN in this game?*

- [ ] Power fantasy — get stronger, do bigger things
- [ ] Competence growth — get better at what you do
- [ ] Horizontal expansion — gain new options, not more power
- [ ] Narrative milestones — advancement marks story beats
- [ ] No advancement — characters are static
