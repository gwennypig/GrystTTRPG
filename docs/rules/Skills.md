# Skills

#ttrpg #rules #skills #abilities #multiclass

## Overview

**Skills** are Gryst's class system — except there are no classes, only skills you invest in.

Every Skill is a **tree of Abilities** (Perks and Gambits) that you unlock by:
1. Increasing your Skill level with **SP (Skill Points)**
2. Spending **AP (Ability Points)** on TRAINED abilities

You can invest in as many Skills as you want. There's no "multiclass penalty" — a Gunslinger-Arcanist-Gambler is a valid build.

---

## Skill Points (SP)

**SP** is the currency for Skill progression.

| Action | Cost |
|--------|------|
| Increase a Skill you have by 1 level | 1 SP |
| Learn a new Skill (first level) | 2 SP |

*Variant: First level costs 1 SP to encourage experimentation.*

**Gaining SP:**
- Character creation
- Milestone rewards from the GM
- Downtime training

---

## Skill Levels & Ability Tiers

As your Skill level increases, you unlock access to higher-tier Abilities:

| Skill Level | Tier | Ability Power |
|-------------|------|---------------|
| 1-2 | Tier 1 | Basic — core actions and simple perks |
| 3-4 | Tier 2 | Intermediate — signature moves |
| 5-6 | Tier 3 | Advanced — powerful, defining abilities |
| 7+ | Mastery | Capstones and legendary options |

---

## Abilities

**Abilities** are the actual things you get from Skills. There are two types:

### Perks (Passive)

Perks are always-on benefits or features:
- "You can see in dim light"
- "+1 to Initiative"
- "You never fail to notice traps"

### Gambits (Active)

Gambits are **actions** — they go into your Gambit library and can be used in play:
- "Fan the Hammer" (attack)
- "Arcane Shield" (defense)
- "Lucky Break" (utility)

*See [[Gambits]] for how Gambits work mechanically.*

---

## Passive vs. TRAINED

### Passive Abilities

**Automatically available** when you meet the Skill level requirement.

> "Gunslinger 1 grants Quick Draw (Perk) — you have it as soon as you have Gunslinger 1."

### TRAINED Abilities

**Require spending AP** to unlock, even if you meet the requirements.

> "Fan the Hammer is TRAINED and costs 2 AP. Even at Gunslinger 3, you don't have it until you spend the AP."

**Why TRAINED?**
- Forces meaningful choices
- Differentiates characters with the same Skills
- Represents dedicated practice beyond basic competence

---

## Ability Points (AP)

**AP** unlocks TRAINED abilities.

| Action | Cost |
|--------|------|
| Unlock a TRAINED ability | 1-3 AP (varies by ability) |

**Gaining AP:**
- Character creation
- Milestone rewards from the GM
- Exceptional roleplay (GM bonus)
- Special in-game training or events

**AP is scarce** — you won't unlock every TRAINED ability in every Skill. This forces identity.

---

## SPARKLE Requirements

Some Abilities have **SPARKLE requirements** in addition to Skill level:

```
Deadeye (Perk) — TRAINED, 2 AP
Requires: Gunslinger 5, PER 4+, Visual (SubSPARKLE) 2+
```

You must meet ALL requirements to unlock the ability:
- Gunslinger at level 5+
- Total Perception at 4+
- At least 2 points specifically in Visual SubSPARKLE

```

══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
✓ Quick Draw (Perk)
  Draw a weapon as a free action.

  Ignore minor penalties to ranged attacks.

TIER 2 (Level 3-4)
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
✓ Covering Fire (Gambit)
  Suppress an area. Add-On Cost: 4.

⚡ Trick Shot (Gambit) — TRAINED, 1 AP
  Shoot around corners, ricochet, show off. Add-On Cost: 5.

⚡ Two-Gun Style (Perk) — TRAINED, 1 AP, Level 4
  Dual-wield ranged weapons without penalty.

TIER 3 (Level 5-6)
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
⚡ Fan the Hammer (Gambit) — TRAINED, 2 AP
  Rapid-fire a revolver. Add-On Cost: 6. Requires AGI 3+.

⚡ Deadeye (Perk) — TRAINED, 2 AP
  +2 to Magnitūdō on ranged attacks. Requires PER 4+.

MASTERY (Level 7+)
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
⚡ One Bullet (Gambit) — TRAINED, 3 AP, Level 7
  A single perfect shot. Automatic Triumphus if it hits.
  Add-On Cost: 10. Once per session.
```

## Building Your Character

### Focused Build
Pick 1-2 Skills and go deep:
- High Skill levels = Tier 3 abilities
- Lots of TRAINED abilities in your specialty
- Risk: narrow, but powerful

### Broad Build  
Pick 3-5 Skills at low levels:
- Lots of Tier 1 abilities
- Flexibility for many situations
- Risk: jack of all trades, master of none

### Hybrid Build
1 deep Skill, 2-3 shallow Skills:
- Signature specialty + utility options
- Most common and recommended

## Skills & Equipment

Skills and [[Modules-and-Loculi|Loculi]] work together:

- **Skills grant Gambits** — inherent abilities you always have
- **Equipment grants Gambits** — abilities tied to your gear

A Gunslinger without a gun still has Quick Draw (the skill to draw fast) but needs a weapon to actually shoot.

Some Skill abilities **modify equipment Gambits**:
> "Deadeye: +2 Magnitūdō on ranged attacks from any weapon"

---

## Custom Skills

Content packs can define new Skills with their own Ability trees. The structure is always:

1. Skill name and theme
2. Tier 1-3 Abilities (Perks and Gambits)
3. Passive vs. TRAINED designation
4. SPARKLE requirements (if any)
5. AP costs for TRAINED abilities

*See GrystForge for the authoring tool.*
